This page shows a project that I am currently developing and continuing to improve.

Tamagotchi GPS Fitness Tracker

I am currently developing a GPS fitness tracker inspired by Tamagotchi. The idea is that the user has a virtual character that levels up as they stay active and complete movement goals.

The project combines fitness tracking, GPS data, and game-like progression to make staying active more interesting.

It is still in development, and I plan to continue improving the design, tracking features, and leveling system.
