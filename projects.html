Projects

These are some of the engineering and science projects I have worked on throughout college.

Martian Loader

The Martian Loader was a robotics project designed to move through a space environment and collect trash. The robot used motors, sensors, and mechanical components to complete the task.

This project helped me practice robotics, engineering design, teamwork, and troubleshooting.

Physics Car Experiment

This physics project focused on testing how the speed and weight of a car affected its motion and performance.

The project involved measurements, calculations, and analyzing results to better understand the relationship between different physical variables.

Lightweight Mouse

This personal project focuses on designing and building a lightweight computer mouse. I am experimenting with reducing weight while still keeping the mouse comfortable and functional.

This project gives me a chance to work with design choices, prototyping, and improving a product through testing.
